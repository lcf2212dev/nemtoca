NemToca Privacy Policy

Version 1.0, November 17, 2026.

This is a translation. If there is any difference between this text and the Portuguese version, the Portuguese version prevails.

In short: NemToca works on your device. It does not declare the internet access permission, has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send data about your calls and your contacts to the developer or to third parties. That data stays on the device, under your control, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

1. Who is responsible

NemToca is provided by Leandro Caetano de Faria, an independent developer, an individual, in Brazil, who publishes on Google Play under the name TriboLab Digital. Contact: contato@tribolabdigital.com.br. This email is the channel to talk about your data and this policy.

2. What the app does not do

  • It does not declare the internet access permission.
  • It has no account or login.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not send the data in sections 3 and 4 to the developer or to third parties.

The developer has no access to the history, lists, rules or contacts on your device.

3. Data handled on the device

The app uses the data below only on your device:

  • Contacts: checked at the time of each call, to know whether the caller is a contact, and during the initial setup, to suggest your most frequent area code. The app does not copy or store your contacts.
  • History of incoming calls: the caller's number, date and time, whether the call rang or was blocked, and the reason. Entries older than 90 days are deleted automatically when a new call is recorded. You can delete entries or the whole history at any time.
  • The number lists and rules you set, including the night mode hours.
  • Preferences, such as theme, language and the blocked-call notice, and app control data: whether the subscription is active and when that was checked, the date you started using this version, and whether the support card was dismissed.

The history and the lists contain numbers of other people, who called you or whom you added.

4. What the data is used for

The data is used only to decide which calls ring, show the history and statistics, send the blocked-call notice and know whether Pro features are unlocked. It is processed to provide the service you asked for by using the app (art. 7, V, of the LGPD). The app does not use this data for advertising or profiling.

5. Permissions

  • Caller ID and spam app role: required to block calls.
  • Contacts: optional. Without it, contacts always ring and the app does not suggest an area code.
  • Notifications: optional. Used for the blocked-call notice.
  • Google Play billing: lets the app open the purchase in the Play Store.

You can revoke the call screening role and the Contacts and Notifications permissions in the Android settings.

6. Purchases

The NemToca Pro subscription and tips are processed by Google Play, with your Google account. To complete and check a purchase, the app receives the order data from the Play Store: the product, the date, the payment status and order identification codes. The app does not receive your name, email or payment details, and it only keeps whether Pro is active and when that was checked. Google's billing library may keep technical records about the purchase on the device; since the app has no internet permission, it does not send them. Purchase data is handled by Google, as an independent controller, under Google's privacy policy, and may be stored outside Brazil.

7. What the developer receives

  • Messages you send to the contact email, with your address and what you write. They are used only to reply and handle your request, and kept for as long as needed for that and to meet legal obligations. The email is hosted by Google, so messages are stored on Google's servers, which may be outside Brazil.
  • Reports Google provides to every developer in the Play Console: sales, orders and refunds, with the product, amount, date and the buyer's region (country, state, postal code and sometimes city), without name or email; public reviews; and aggregated crash and performance statistics from people who agreed to share diagnostics with Google. The app does not send this data; Google generates it on its own. It is used only to manage sales and refunds and to fix the app.

8. Backup and deletion

Android automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your data does not go to the cloud. Direct copy to a new device is also turned off: when you switch phones, you set the app up again. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the Android settings, deletes everything it stored. The Android call log itself may still show declined calls, because it belongs to the system, not to the app.

9. Security

The data stays in the app's private storage on the device, which other apps cannot access. The app does not use its own encryption: protection depends on the screen lock and on Android's encryption. The blocked-call notice shows the number on the screen, including on the lock screen if Android is set up that way.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children.

11. Your rights (LGPD)

The data on the device stays with you, and you control it directly:

  • view the history, lists and rules in the app itself;
  • delete history entries or the whole history;
  • revoke permissions in the Android settings;
  • uninstall the app to delete everything.

For the data the developer receives (section 7), you can ask for confirmation, access, correction, deletion and the other measures provided for in art. 18 of the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Law No. 13,709/2018), by writing to contato@tribolabdigital.com.br. Requests are answered within 15 days. You can also file a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. The current version and its date are at the top of this text. Relevant changes are announced in the app before they apply.
