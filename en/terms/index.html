2. What the app does

NemToca is a call blocker. To work, it must be chosen as the Android caller ID and spam app. When an incoming call reaches the app, before it rings, the app decides, on the device itself, whether it rings or is declined, according to the rules you set:

  • allowed area codes (DDDs), or the option to allow all;
  • number types: other area codes, no area code, international, 0800, 0303, 0300, 0500, 0900, 4004, 4003, 3003 and short codes;
  • the "Even if saved in contacts" option;
  • the "Always allowed" and "Always blocked" lists;
  • a main switch that turns blocking on and off.

The app also offers "Test a number", history, statistics and an optional blocked-call notice. When in doubt, for example when the call has no number or there is an error reading the data or the contacts, the call rings.

3. Limits of the service

Please read carefully what the app does not do and cannot guarantee:

  • The result depends on the rules you choose. The app may block a call you wanted to receive or let an unwanted call through. Check your rules from time to time. The history shows the reason for each decision.
  • Calls from hidden or private numbers arrive without a number. The app cannot block them, and they ring.
  • Without the Contacts permission, Android does not pass calls from people in your contacts to the app. Those calls always ring, even if the number is in "Always blocked".
  • How the app works depends on Android and on the device manufacturer. Some manufacturers restrict apps running in the background. Uninterrupted operation is not guaranteed.
  • The app does not block calls you make, SMS or messages.
  • The app is not an emergency service and does not replace official channels, such as Não Me Perturbe or Procon.
